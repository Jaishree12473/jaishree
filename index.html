🌤️ 1. Seasonal Superiority

Kharif yields the highest net profitability (₹1,78,914.65) and highest yield (5.63 t/ha), driven by monsoon rainfall. In contrast, Zaid experiences negative margins (-₹24,804.82) due to high water deficit and excessive irrigation costs in summer.

🎋 2. Cash Crops vs. Cereals

Sugarcane (₹8,17,187.99) and Chilli (₹7,50,878.34) lead all crops in economic returns. Staple cereals like Wheat and Rice showed negative margins under commercial cost structures, demonstrating the necessity of MSP and input subsidies.

💧 3. Irrigation Efficiency

Drip irrigation generated the highest profit (₹2,19,626.00). Conversely, Flood irrigation proved the most wasteful, consuming an average of 8,026.47 m³ of water with the lowest productivity score (3.44 t/1000 m³).

🚜 4. Regional Champions

Punjab (₹1,36,025.64) and Maharashtra (₹1,35,428.86) recorded the highest state-level profitability, supported by disciplined nutrient management and irrigation infrastructure.

🔍 5. Primary Yield Drivers

Multivariate correlation demonstrates that Water Efficiency is the strongest positive determinant of agricultural yield, while total Revenue serves as the primary linear determinant of farmer profit.

🛡️ 6. Policy Recommendations

Subsidize conversion from flood to micro-irrigation (drip/sprinkler), enhance pest surveillance during humid Kharif cycles, and encourage mixed cash-crop rotation to protect cereal farmers.
